Get your store ready

Sign in, set up your catalog and checkout, and run through a customer order before launch.

Sign inAdd your first productsConfigure checkoutWalk through a test order

Sign in

  1. Open your invitation and use the email your store was set up with. If the link asks you to create an account or enter a one-time code, follow it and verify your email.
  2. Sign in on your store’s dashboard address. If you use the shared platform, open RCM Commerce sign-in.
  3. Open Settings → General and check your store name, contact information, business address, and time zone.
Use the email on your invitationIf your account opens without a store, ask the person who invited you to confirm which address they used.

Add your first products

  1. Open Products → Add product. Start with a recognizable name, clear photos, and a short description explaining what’s included.
  2. Give each sellable option its own variant, such as Small or Large. Check the price and stock for every variant. Use inventory tracking for stock you physically count.
  3. Group products in Products → Collections. Use the names shoppers will look for, like Tees or New arrivals.
  4. Keep products in Draft until you’re ready. Switch them to Active to let a connected website show them.
Start with one productCheck its image, size options, price, stock, and collection on the actual website before entering the whole catalog.

Configure checkout

  1. Open Settings → Payments and use the Stripe connection offered for your store. Verify the dashboard shows card payments are ready before taking real payments.
  2. Set your delivery regions, shipping rates, and pickup locations under Settings → Shipping and delivery. Review tax settings with the person who handles your store’s taxes.
  3. Fill in Settings → Policies and Settings → Notifications. Check what shoppers will see on the checkout page and in the order email.
  4. Review Settings → Checkout for contact fields, payment methods and whether online checkout is paused.

Walk through a test order

  1. Use a test store or test payment setup. Open the website in a private window and start from a product page.
  2. Pick a variant, add it to the cart, and check shipping, taxes and the final total at checkout.
  3. Complete the test payment. Find the order in Orders and confirm the order status and stock change.
  4. Test the confirmation email and, if you ship orders, fulfillment and tracking. Try the same flow on a phone.
