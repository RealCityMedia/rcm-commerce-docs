Update products and inventory

Open a product to change its description, images, or options. Review every variant after adding sizes or colours. Use Inventory to record counts and stock adjustments, and Collections to reorganize the storefront.

StatusUse it for
DraftWork in progress or a future release.
ActiveAn item ready for a connected storefront to display.
ArchivedAn older product you want to retain in the dashboard.
Check the storefront after product changesA connected site may cache catalog responses. Refresh the page and verify that the product is Active and in the intended collection.

Process and fulfill orders

  1. Open Orders and read the payment and fulfillment status. A pending manual payment may require confirmation before shipping.
  2. Check the customer’s delivery or pickup details and line items. Pack the items before recording fulfillment.
  3. For shipments, enter the carrier and tracking information. If the order needs multiple shipments, fulfill the items included in each one.
  4. For returns or cancellations, open the order and use its available refund or cancellation action. Review the amount, notification, and restock choice before confirming.

Check payments, shipping, and messages

  • Use Payments to review available Stripe activity. Check connection status under Settings → Payments if customers cannot pay online.
  • Change pickup or shipping under Settings → Shipping and delivery. Test a typical checkout address after changing a zone or rate.
  • Use Messages to reply to conversations from your connected website. See website integrations if your site needs a chat widget.

Invite staff

Use Settings → Team to manage access for the people who help run your store. Review their role when they join and remove access when they leave. The owner controls settings like website key replacement and public collaboration visibility.
