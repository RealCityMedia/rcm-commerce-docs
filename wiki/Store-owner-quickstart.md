# Store owner quickstart

1. Sign in with the email from your invitation. Verify your email if prompted.
2. In **Settings → General**, confirm the store name, business address and contact details.
3. In **Products**, add clear photos, variants, prices and stock. Start with a draft and activate it when ready.
4. Connect your payment account, configure delivery and pickup, and write your policies.
5. Run a test checkout, verify the order and confirmation email, and test shipping and tracking.

[Read the full setup walkthrough](https://lilman514.github.io/rcm-commerce-docs/getting-started.html)

## Showcase collaborations

Create an entry in **Products → Collaborations**. Add its image, product name and company. Use a matching subsection name to group related entries. Choose Grid, List, or Featured in **Settings → Website → Collaboration layouts** and copy the embed. Turn on visibility in **Settings → General → Collaborations** when you are ready to show the showcase.
