Quick checks for sign-in, missing products, payments, embeds, and storefront requests.

Sign-in and access

My invitation opens the wrong account

Sign out and open the invitation in a private window. Enter the email address the invitation was sent to. If it has expired or the address has changed, ask your store owner to confirm access.

My settings are read-only

Check your role in the store. Owners manage store-wide settings such as keys and the showcase switch.

Products, checkout, and shipments

A product is missing from my site

Make sure the product is Active, assigned to the collection the website uses, and available under its intended variant. Ask your developer to confirm the website uses the correct store key and to check cache freshness.

Customers cannot complete checkout

Check the checkout pause switch, Stripe connection status, and whether shipping or pickup is available for the customer’s address. Repeat the flow on a test storefront and record the exact error.

A customer cannot look up their order

Confirm the reference and the email or phone number originally used. Check the tracking number in the dashboard if the parcel is already shipped.

Embeds and API requests

The collaboration embed is empty

Check the visibility switch, at least one saved entry, the full publishable key, and any subsection filter. A site builder may require a custom HTML block that permits scripts. A Content Security Policy must allow requests to the RCM origin.

An image does not load

Verify the image URL responds and can be shown on another website, or upload the image through the dashboard. Developers should resolve paths starting with /media/ against the RCM API origin.

My integration returns 401 or 403

Check the API origin and full X-RCM-Key. A rotated key stops working immediately. Server-only operations require a server key and belong on your backend.

When asking for helpInclude the store name, time of the issue, page URL, operation, and the exact message. Keep full keys and shopper details out of screenshots and support posts.
